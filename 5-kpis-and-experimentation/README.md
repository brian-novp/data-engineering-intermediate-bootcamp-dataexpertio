# TOKOPEDIA’S KPI AND EXPERIMENTATION
The rise of online marketplace in Indonesia changed how people are buying goods and gadgets. Before online marketplace, they buy and research things they want to buy by going to the local stores and inquiring with the store assistants. Now, the customers prefer to find information first or buy it online through an online marketplace. At the time of this writing there are some online marketplaces in Indonesia and one of the widely used online marketplace  is Tokopedia. Personally, I use Tokopedia as my sole platform of e-commerce where I buy so many things: food ingredients, seasoning, computer parts, cellphone, car parts, accessories , even paying some recurring monthly bill and many more. 

## Experiment 1: Tokopedia's Phone Bill Bundling vs BPJS Kesehatan Bundling - A Revenue Experiment
**Objective:** Everybody loves convenience. Especially when it involves paying a recurrent monthly bill. This is a valid pain point for Tokopedia users and we can provide relief by bundling two kinds of products. By buying x times or y IDR threshold monthly, we provide a discount or cashback for the transaction fee of each bill or the bill itself. Users can choose one bundle per month (Phone Bill or BPJS Kesehatan bill). The goal is to see if Phone Bill Bundling performs better than BPJS Kesehatan Bundling in terms of Transaction Fee Revenue.

**Null Hypothesis:** Phone Bill Bundling does not perform better than BPJS kesehatan one in terms of transaction fee revenue.

**Alternative Hypothesis:** Phone Bill Bundling performs better than BPJS kesehatan one in terms of transaction fee revenue

**Leading Metric:** Increase  Average Order Value and e-commerce conversion rates (sale).

**Lagging Metric:** Increase in Transaction Fee Revenue.

**Test cell allocation:** 50%-50%

## Experiment 2: Faster load page time and clear description   
**Objective:**  Nobody likes to wait for a product page to load slowly. Much less: an ambiguous product description with little or too much text for the customer to read. Ignoring these two will only bring higher bounce rate and exit rate and thus affect conversion rate, ultimately damaging revenue. Faster load page time means compressed pictures and simpler to no animation (if any) so that users with slow internet connection and budget devices can find what they want and do business with us. Slower load page time means high density pixels in pictures and smooth animation. The goal is to see if faster load page time in product page (xx ms) and clear description (min of x words and max of y words)

**Null Hypothesis:** Faster load page time and clear description in product page does not perform better than slower load page time and ambiguous description in terms of conversion rate.

**Alternative Hypothesis:** Faster load page time and clear description in product page perform better than slower load page time and ambiguous description in terms of conversion rate (higher).

**Leading Metric:** lower bounce rate overall and exit rate in product page

**Lagging Metric:** Increase Conversion rate

**Test cell allocation:** 50%-50%

This is a valid point for users who do not want to register because they may have trust issues with marketing spam.
